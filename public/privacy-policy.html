Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 September 2026

CareerGyan respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through this event registration website may be collected, used and protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for the CareerGyan Abroad Education Fair, we may collect your full name, email address, mobile number, preferred country of study, course and education level. We may also receive payment-related identifiers from our payment provider when you complete a registration payment.

2. How We Use Information

  • To process and confirm your event registration.
  • To process and verify payments.
  • To send registration, payment and event-related communications.
  • To respond to questions and provide relevant event or education-service assistance.
  • To maintain records and prevent fraud or misuse.

3. Payments

Payments are processed through Razorpay. CareerGyan does not request or store your full card, UPI, banking or other payment credentials on this website. Payment information is handled by the payment provider according to its own policies.

4. reCAPTCHA

This website uses Google reCAPTCHA to help prevent automated or abusive submissions. Its use is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

5. Email Communications

Registration emails may be sent to the email address supplied in the form. We may also send administrative notifications related to the registration.

6. Data Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers that are necessary to operate the registration process, such as payment, database, security and email-service providers. We do not sell registration information.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect registration information. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Retention

Registration information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for event administration, accounting, customer support, legal obligations and legitimate business records.

9. Your Choices

If you have questions about your registration information or want to request correction of inaccurate information, contact CareerGyan using the contact details provided on the main website.

10. Updates

This policy may be updated when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
